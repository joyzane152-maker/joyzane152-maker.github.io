Good to know

Your questions, answered

What's the difference between a maintenance clean and a deep clean?

A maintenance clean keeps your home fresh week to week. A deep clean tackles every area of the room and takes much more time, using the correct products one at a time, from degreasers to limescale, mould and stain removers.

Can you help my mum or dad?

Yes. I visit first to meet them, explain everything and follow up with the agreed plan in writing. I can help with housekeeping and home management as well as cleaning.

Are you insured and DBS checked?

Yes, I'm fully insured and enhanced DBS checked.

Will it always be the same cleaner?

Always. I'm a solo cleaner, so it's me every visit.

Do you do end of tenancy cleans?

Yes, including move in cleans for new tenants. Get in touch as early as you can and I'll do my best to fit you in.
